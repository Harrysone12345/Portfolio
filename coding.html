COD-01 / RESEARCH

NN + Gaussian Process Surrogate for Hypersonic Air Chemistry

A feedforward network (built from scratch with NumPy backprop) maps temperature to equilibrium mole fractions of 5-species high-temperature air, with a per-species Gaussian Process layered on top for uncertainty bands, built to speed up reentry heat-transfer modeling. Trained on 1,470 CFD solver outputs, with the data preprocessed and the architecture and hyperparameters tuned to minimize prediction error across the equilibrium composition space.
